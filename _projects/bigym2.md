BiGym 2.0 is a humanoid household manipulation benchmark with 20 tasks on the Unitree G1, 60 human VR demonstrations per task, and one shared whole-body controller. Learned policies and coding agents are scored on the same hidden seeds, and a cold-start coding agent performs comparably to learned baselines. Under review.
